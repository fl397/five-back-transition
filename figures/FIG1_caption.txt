Figure 1. Defensive numerical recovery is gradual: all five defenders return after a median 5.63 s, while rim access decreases as more defenders recover. Main panel: share of transition possessions (after a defensive rebound or a live-ball turnover) in which all five defenders have returned to their own half of the court, by time since the possession started (2015-16 NBA, 631 games, 31,794 possessions tracked for 8 s). Dashed line and shaded band: median (5.63 s) and interquartile range (4.82-6.39 s) of the time until all five are back; 10.4% of possessions still lack a fifth defender at 8 s. Grey line (right axis): mean number of defenders back. Inset: difference in the probability that the first field-goal attempt is a rim attempt (within 4 ft) when two or fewer, three, or four defenders are back, relative to all five back, from a linear probability model that adjusts for elapsed possession time, start type, period and score margin; bars are 95% confidence intervals with standard errors clustered by game. Values are associations, not causal effects.
